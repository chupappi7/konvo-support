How much does Konvo cost?

Konvo Premium unlocks every drill, goal and method, monthly or yearly. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free. Prices for your country are shown in the app before you subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Konvo AI, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the paywall in Konvo and tap Restore. Make sure you're signed in with the Apple ID you subscribed with.

Who am I talking to in the drills?

AI personas. Their replies and Sage's coaching are generated by AI, so they can be wrong or odd sometimes. They are practice partners, not real people and not therapy. See the privacy policy for how your messages are handled.

How do I report a bug?

Email support@konvoai.app with what happened and your iPhone model. We reply within 1–2 business days.
