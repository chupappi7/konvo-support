SUPPORT & FAQ

Got a question?

If you don't see your answer here, email us — we read every message.

How do I contact support?
Email support@konvoai.app and we'll reply within 1–2 business days. You can also send feedback or report bugs from inside the app via Settings → Report a bug.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → tap Konvo AI → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your next renewal to avoid being charged. Your premium access stays active until the end of the current period.
Can I get a refund?
All purchases are handled by Apple, not us. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com and select your Konvo charge. Apple decides refunds based on their policy — we don't have the ability to issue refunds directly.
Does Konvo work offline?
Most of the app works offline — the library, your plan, and method details are all on-device. The AI drill conversations require an internet connection because the AI persona and coaching responses are generated server-side.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open Konvo on the new device, sign in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. Your subscription will sync automatically.
Is my conversation data private?
Yes. Your drill conversations are sent to the AI for coaching but are not used to train AI models and are not sold to anyone. Your plan, progress, and personal info live on your device. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
Why does my drill say "Practicing — Hold a longer conversation"?
Each drill is tied to a goal in your personalized plan. The banner reminds you which skill you're working on right now. You can switch goals anytime from the Home screen.
The app crashed / something looks wrong.
Open Settings → Report a bug inside the app. The form pre-fills with diagnostic info that helps us fix it faster. Or email support@konvoai.app with a screenshot and what you were doing when it broke.
Do you have an Android version?
Not yet. Konvo is iPhone-only for now. If you want to be notified when Android lands, email support@konvoai.app with "Android" in the subject.

Still stuck?

The fastest way to get help is to email us directly. We're one indie developer who actually reads everything.
